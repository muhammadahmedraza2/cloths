Privacy and Policies

Data Usage

All data entered into Clothing ERP (customers, suppliers, products, transactions) is used strictly for business operations within your organization.

Authorization Workflow

Records created in any Setup module remain in "UnAuthorize" status until reviewed and approved by an authorized user, ensuring a maker-checker control on master data.

Data Storage

This demo build stores data locally in your browser for evaluation purposes. A production deployment would connect to your secured backend and database.

Contact

For any privacy-related concerns, please contact your system administrator.
